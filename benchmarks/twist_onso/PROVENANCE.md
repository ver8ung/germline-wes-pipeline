## Reading these numbers

- **PASS** rows score only records that passed hard filtering; **ALL** rows
  score every record. Both matter: a large ALL-to-PASS recall drop means the
  filters are discarding true variants.
- Recall here is bounded by capture coverage, not only by the caller. Truth
  variants in target regions the capture never covered count as false
  negatives however good the calling is. Use `dev/diag_capture.sh` to split
  the false negatives into covered and uncovered before drawing conclusions.
- The evaluation region is the capture BED intersected with the GIAB
  high-confidence regions, so numbers are NOT comparable across runs that
  used different capture kits or different truth sets -- the denominators
  differ. Compare ALL-to-PASS BEHAVIOUR across runs, not absolute recall.
- With stratification on, `Subset=*` is the headline row and the named
  subsets are diagnostic: they say WHERE calling fails, not just how often.
  See `happy.extended.csv`.
- Scored with the **vcfeval** (RTG) comparison engine rather than hap.py's
  default `xcmp`. Measured on identical input, the two agree exactly on SNPs
  and to within ~0.001 F1 on indels: hap.py already left-shifts and decomposes
  both callsets before comparing (`preprocessing_leftshift`), so most
  variant-representation differences are normalised before either engine sees
  them. vcfeval is used because it is the stricter, more widely cited engine,
  NOT because it materially changes these numbers.
